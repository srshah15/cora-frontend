CORA

Turn a protocol into an operational plan

Upload a clinical research protocol PDF. The assistant pulls out the visit schedule, procedures, staffing, safety reporting, billing triggers and resource needs, then shows a draft for you to review.

Development build. Only upload publicly available protocols (e.g. from ClinicalTrials.gov). Do not upload confidential institutional or sponsor documents.

Processing protocol

  1. IngestionExtracting text from the PDF, OCR if needed
  2. ExtractionPulling each operational category from the text
  3. StructuringMapping results into the standard schema
  4. Review checkFlagging low-confidence items for a human
